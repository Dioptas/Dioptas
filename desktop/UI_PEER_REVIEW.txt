## Optional suggestions (not defects)
- **Map vs. Integration gutter:** with no pattern, Map now disables Log, √, AA and A. Integration keeps the same local display toggles enabled (`IntegrationPlotControls.tsx:342-396`), which lets you set them ahead of time. You could keep those four enabled in Map and disable only the controls that need data (unit conversion, ROI, edit windows).
- **Repeated message:** with no calibration, Map says it three times: in the empty state, in the task-panel note (`MapControls.tsx:236-240`) and in the status bar. The task-panel note could go.
- **Legend placement:** in `batch-normalization-compact-large.png` the movable legend sits on top of the green trace. It could start in the corner with the least data. Moving legends is your choice, so this is a starting-position suggestion only.
- **Disabled Apply:** a tooltip on the greyed-out Apply button, such as "Limits unchanged", would say why it is disabled.
- **Batch heatmap axis:** the vertical axis has no visible "Frame" label. It is covered by the accessible description, the hover readout and "Frame 1 of 6", which fits your no-extra-labels choice.
